About RRB CBT 2 Mathematics

Mathematics is the highest-weighted section in the RRB CBT 2 paper, with about 35 of the 100 questions. The questions test Class 10 level arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry and basic statistics. Because every question carries equal marks and a wrong answer costs one-third of a mark, accuracy and speed matter more than attempting hard problems.

This page gives you a practice bank with worked solutions, followed by a formula sheet, shortcut methods, a topic-wise weightage table and a week-by-week plan. Attempt the questions first, then read the explanation for every one you got wrong or took more than a minute to solve.

Shortcut Tricks to Save Time

1. Use the multiplication-factor method for percentages

A 20% increase is multiplying by 1.2 and a 25% decrease is multiplying by 0.75. For successive changes, simply multiply the factors. This turns a long percentage chain into one multiplication and is the fastest way to solve discount and price-change questions.

2. Convert fractions you use often

Memorise 1/2 = 50%, 1/3 = 33.33%, 1/4 = 25%, 1/5 = 20%, 1/6 = 16.67%, 1/7 = 14.28%, 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/9 = 11.11% and 1/10 = 10%. Most percentage and ratio problems become mental calculations once these are automatic.

3. Treat work questions as man-days

Assign the total work a convenient number such as the LCM of the given days. If A takes 18 days and B takes 12 days, take the work as 36 units. A does 2 units a day and B does 3, so together they do 5 units a day. This removes fractions completely.

4. Use options to verify

In age, ratio and equation problems, substitute the answer options back into the condition. Starting with the middle option often lets you eliminate two choices at once.

5. Check units before calculating

Many mistakes in speed and mensuration questions come from mixing km/h with m/s, or cm with m. Convert everything first, then calculate.

Preparation Strategy for Mathematics

  • Week 1: Revise number system, percentage, ratio, average and profit-loss. Solve at least 20 questions a day.
  • Week 2: Cover time-speed-distance, time-work, pipes, boats and interest. Write each formula on a card and revise daily.
  • Week 3: Study algebra, geometry, mensuration and trigonometry. Practise drawing figures for every geometry problem.
  • Week 4: Take full mock tests, limit Mathematics to 35 minutes, and keep an error log of every mistake with the correct method.

Common mistakes to avoid

Rushing the first read of the question, forgetting the 1/3 negative mark, guessing when no option can be eliminated, and spending more than 90 seconds on a single question are the four most frequent reasons for lost marks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Mathematics questions come in RRB CBT 2?

Typically about 35 questions out of 100, making it the single largest section of the paper.

What is the difficulty level?

The level is around Class 10 standard, with some questions requiring two or three steps. Speed is the main challenge, not advanced concepts.

How much time should I spend on Mathematics?

Aim for about 35 minutes of the 90-minute paper, which leaves time for Reasoning, General Science and General Awareness.

Is there negative marking?

Yes, one-third of a mark is deducted for every wrong answer. Unattempted questions carry no penalty.

How should I use this question bank?

Attempt each question yourself before opening the solution. Mark the ones you missed and re-attempt them after three days.
