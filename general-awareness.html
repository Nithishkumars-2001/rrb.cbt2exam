About RRB CBT 2 General Awareness

General Awareness carries about 15 of the 100 questions in RRB CBT 2. It covers current affairs, Indian polity, history, geography, economy, sports, awards and railway-specific knowledge. It is the most scoring section for candidates who revise regularly, because every answer is a single fact that takes only seconds to recall.

This page offers a practice bank with explanations, followed by quick-reference tables on railway zones, important days and key facts. Read the explanation for every question, not only the ones you missed, since the notes often contain a second fact that is asked in the exam.

How to Prepare General Awareness

  • Current affairs: Read a monthly digest and note only facts that can become questions, such as names, places, dates and numbers.
  • Static GK: Study with NCERT books of Class 6 to 10 for history and geography, and Laxmikanth-style summaries for polity.
  • Revision: Keep a small notebook of one-line facts and revise it every night for ten minutes.
  • Practice: Attempt 20 questions daily and re-read the explanation of each wrong answer.
  • Time in exam: Spend no more than 10 minutes on this section. If you do not know the answer immediately, move on.

Note on current affairs questions

Questions that mention offices such as ministers or chairpersons can change with time. Always verify them against a recent official source before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many General Awareness questions come in RRB CBT 2?

Roughly 15 questions out of 100.

Is General Awareness difficult?

It is factual rather than analytical, so steady revision gives high accuracy in little time.

How many months of current affairs should I read?

The previous 8 to 12 months before the exam date is sufficient.

Is there negative marking?

Yes, one-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer, so avoid blind guessing.
