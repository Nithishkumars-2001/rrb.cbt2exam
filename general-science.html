About RRB CBT 2 General Science

General Science carries about 20 of the 100 questions in RRB CBT 2. The paper tests Physics, Chemistry and Biology at Class 9 and 10 level, with a few questions on everyday science and technology. Because most questions are direct and conceptual, this section can be finished in around 10 minutes by a well-prepared candidate.

Use the question bank below first, then revise the reference tables that follow. Units, vitamins, common chemical names and disease causes are asked repeatedly, so these tables are worth memorising.

How to Prepare General Science

  • Use NCERT: Read Class 9 and 10 Science books fully once, focusing on boxed facts and chapter summaries.
  • Learn tables: Memorise units, vitamins, diseases and chemical names. These give quick, certain marks.
  • Understand concepts: For physics, know the formula and what each symbol means rather than only the number.
  • Practise daily: Attempt 20 questions a day and note the ones you missed in a revision notebook.
  • Link with railways: Topics such as electric traction, friction and brakes, and metals are frequently framed in a railway context.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many General Science questions come in RRB CBT 2?

Usually about 20 questions out of 100.

What is the difficulty level?

Class 9 and 10 standard. Most questions are direct and need one fact or formula.

Which subject is most important?

The three subjects are balanced, but Physics numericals and Biology of the human body repeat most often.

Is NCERT enough?

Yes. NCERT Class 9 and 10 plus regular practice questions cover the large majority of the paper.
