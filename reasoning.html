About RRB CBT 2 Reasoning

General Intelligence and Reasoning carries about 30 of the 100 questions in RRB CBT 2, making it the second-largest section after Mathematics. It tests analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, ranking, syllogism, calendar and clock problems.

Reasoning rewards pattern recognition and speed. Practise the question bank below, then study the rule sheets that follow. Once you know the standard patterns, most questions can be solved in under 40 seconds.

Shortcut Tricks for Reasoning

1. Solve analogy by finding the relation first

Write the relation in a sentence, such as "doctor works in hospital", then apply the same sentence to the second pair. Avoid choosing an option just because the words feel related.

2. Use letter positions for coding

Write the positions of the first word and compare with the coded word. The shift may be constant, increasing, or alternate. Check at least two letters before applying the rule to the full word.

3. Draw for relations and directions

Never solve blood relation or direction questions mentally. A quick diagram with arrows and gender symbols prevents most mistakes.

4. Eliminate for odd-one-out

Look for the category first: type, use, shape or number property. If three items share a feature that the fourth lacks, the fourth is the answer.

Preparation Strategy for Reasoning

  • Week 1: Analogy, classification, series. Practise 30 questions a day to build speed.
  • Week 2: Coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, ranking.
  • Week 3: Syllogism, clock, calendar, dice, counting figures, Venn diagrams.
  • Week 4: Timed sectional tests. Aim to finish 30 questions in 25 minutes.

Common mistakes to avoid

Reading options before understanding the question, skipping a diagram, confusing "from left" with "from right", and spending more than a minute on one question are the usual reasons for lost marks.
